SHEET 02 — THE FIRM

Engineering built on accuracy

Established in 2014 in Thane, Maharashtra, Meline Technology Solution Pvt Ltd is an engineering design and detailing firm serving a USA-based client across equipment design and process piping detailing for the oil & gas, petrochemical and process industries.

  • — Mechanical, structural, electrical & instrumentation design
  • — Field site support with precise as-built dimensioning
  • — Design validity checks & drawing conversion
  • — Installation, testing & commissioning supervision
