About Us

Engineering Solutions Built on Accuracy & Trust

Meline Technology Solution Pvt Ltd is an engineering design and detailing firm based in Thane, Maharashtra — established in 2014 with a single objective: deliver dependable mechanical, structural, electrical and instrumentation engineering at competitive prices.

We support a USA-based company with equipment design and process piping design & detailing, specifically for the oil & gas, petrochemical and process industries. Our services span 3D modeling, stress analysis, as-built documentation, design validity checks, and model/drawing conversion — all delivered using industry-standard CAD packages.

  • ✓ Mechanical, structural, electrical & instrumentation design
  • ✓ Field site support with precise as-built dimensioning
  • ✓ Design validity checks & drawing conversion
  • ✓ Installation, testing & commissioning supervision

What We Do

Comprehensive Engineering Services

From concept to commissioning — our multidisciplinary team supports every stage of your project lifecycle with precision and care.

3D Modeling & Structures

Detailed 3D models of skids, structures, platforms and piping systems built for accuracy and manufacturability.

Piping Design & Detailing

Process piping design, isometric drawings and manufacturing bills of material for oil & gas and petrochemical facilities.

Mechanical & Structural Design

Cost-effective structural and mechanical engineering — from base frames to skid layouts — validated for real-world loads.

Electrical & Instrumentation

Design, supply, testing and commissioning of power control centers, MCCs and field instrumentation systems.

Site Services & As-Built Surveys

Installation, Testing & Commissioning

Supervision of mechanical, electrical and instrumentation installation, pre-commissioning, SAT and O&M support.
